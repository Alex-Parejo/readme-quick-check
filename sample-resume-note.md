# Sample Resume Improvement Note

## Target Role

Customer Support Specialist

## Main Gap

The resume describes tasks, but it does not show customer impact, tools used, or measurable outcomes.

## Suggested Rewrite

Before:

```text
Answered customer questions and helped with support tickets.
```

After:

```text
Resolved customer support tickets across email and chat, improving response clarity and helping users complete account, billing, and product setup tasks without escalation.
```

## Why This Works

- Starts with a clear action verb.
- Names the support channels.
- Shows the type of customer problems handled.
- Keeps the wording ATS-friendly without inventing metrics.
